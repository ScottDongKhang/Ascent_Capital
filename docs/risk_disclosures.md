## 1. Past Performance

Past performance is not indicative of future results. The strategy's historical backtested and walk-forward simulated returns do not guarantee similar future performance. All return figures are presented net of estimated transaction costs unless stated otherwise. Simulated performance has inherent limitations and may not reflect actual results achievable in live trading.

## 2. Systematic Strategy Risk

Ascent Capital is a fully systematic, algorithmic trading strategy. Systematic strategies may fail without warning when market dynamics shift or when their underlying statistical assumptions break down. The strategy was developed using historical data from 2020–2026 and may underperform or incur significant losses in market conditions structurally different from this period.

## 3. Leverage

This strategy does not use leverage. Maximum gross exposure is 100% of NAV. The SPY 200-day moving average overlay may reduce gross exposure to 70% during adverse market conditions.

## 4. Liquidity Risk

The strategy holds approximately 15 positions in US equities, fixed income ETFs, commodities, international equities, and alternative assets. In periods of severe market stress, positions may be difficult to liquidate at quoted prices. The strategy imposes automatic exposure reduction (kill switch at −15% drawdown) to manage liquidity risk.

## 5. Model Risk

All alpha signals are derived from historical price data, fundamental data, SEC filings, earnings transcripts, and alternative data sources. These signals may not predict future returns. The machine learning component (XGBoost) is periodically retrained but may overfit to historical patterns. Factor models used for portfolio construction are estimates subject to estimation error.

## 6. Concentration Risk

The strategy holds approximately 15 positions with a maximum single-position size of 10% of NAV. Loss of any single position has material impact on portfolio performance. The strategy is diversified across sectors but may have concentrated factor exposures.

## 7. AI and Large Language Model Risk

This strategy employs large language models (LLMs) as advisory components in its debate layer and as alpha signal generators (LLM Fundamental sleeve). LLM outputs are not guaranteed to be accurate, consistent, or free of hallucination. All LLM components are advisory only and do not directly control execution without quantitative validation and human oversight.

## 8. Execution Risk

The strategy uses algorithmic order execution. Actual fills may differ from estimated prices due to market impact, slippage, and execution timing. Large orders use TWAP execution (currently kill-switched off pending validation).

## 9. Technology Risk

The strategy relies on automated systems, third-party data providers (Yahoo Finance, FRED, EDGAR, Alpaca Markets), and cloud infrastructure. System failures, data provider outages, or API changes may prevent timely order execution or cause unintended trades.
